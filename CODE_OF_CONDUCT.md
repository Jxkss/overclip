# Code of Conduct

Overclip is a small, friendly project. Everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests, discussions
or anywhere else the project lives — is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep it welcoming for people of every background and experience level.
- Give constructive, specific feedback; accept it gracefully.
- Not harass, insult, or post discriminatory or sexual content, and not share anyone's private
  information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who don't follow these
rules. To report a problem privately, open a
[security advisory](https://github.com/Jxkss/overclip/security/advisories/new) or contact the
maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
